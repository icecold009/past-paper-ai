## Representative Examples
### Paper 1
- Example 1 (P1): The function f is defined by f(x) = 2x² − 8x + 5. Express f(x) in completed square form and hence state the minimum value and the value of x at which it occurs. [4]
- Example 2 (P1): Find the equation of the circle with centre (3, −2) that passes through the point (7, 1). [3]
- Example 3 (P1): The first term of a geometric series is 6 and the common ratio is ⅔. Find the sum to infinity and the smallest value of n for which the sum of the first n terms exceeds 17.5. [5]
- Example 4 (P1): Differentiate with respect to x: y = (3x² + 1)⁴. Hence find the gradient of the curve at x = 1. [4]
- Example 5 (P1): A curve has equation y = x³ − 6x² + 9x + 2. Find the coordinates of the stationary points and determine their nature. [6]
- Example 6 (P1): The angle θ satisfies 2sin²θ + sinθ − 1 = 0 for 0° ≤ θ ≤ 360°. Find all solutions. [4]

### Paper 5
- Example 1 (P5): The heights of plants are normally distributed with mean 25 cm and standard deviation 4 cm. Find the probability that a randomly chosen plant has height greater than 30 cm. [3]
- Example 2 (P5): In how many ways can a committee of 3 be chosen from 8 people if a specific person must be included? [3]
- Example 3 (P5): A discrete random variable X has the distribution P(X = x) = kx for x = 1, 2, 3, 4. Find k, E(X), and Var(X). [6]
- Example 4 (P5): A bag contains 5 red and 3 blue balls. Two balls are drawn without replacement. Find the probability that both are the same colour. [3]
- Example 5 (P5): The following data gives the times (minutes) taken by 10 students to complete a task: 12, 15, 11, 18, 14, 20, 13, 17, 16, 19. Calculate the mean and standard deviation. Draw a stem-and-leaf diagram. [5]
